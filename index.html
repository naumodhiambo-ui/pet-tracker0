Example readings

Installable PWA

On supported browsers, install SmartPet from the browser menu. On iPhone or iPad, use Safari’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.

Demo limitations: This site does not connect to a tracker or collect live location, health, or activity data. Device pairing, sign-in, syncing, and real commands are not available. Do not use it for pet safety decisions.
